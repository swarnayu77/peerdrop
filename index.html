How PeerDrop Works

1. Pure Peer-to-Peer Architecture

Unlike cloud storage services, files transferred via PeerDrop never upload to a cloud server or disk. Data is streamed in-flight directly from device RAM to device RAM over encrypted WebRTC DataChannels.

2. End-to-End Cryptography

All payloads are encrypted with standard DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security) and SRTP. Neither third parties nor intermediaries can intercept or tamper with your files.

3. Flow-Controlled 64KB Chunking

Large video files, high-res photos, and deep nested folders are segmented into 64KB chunks with backpressure monitoring (`bufferedAmountLow`) to avoid browser crashes or buffer bloat.

4. Local Wi-Fi Speed

When two devices are connected on the same local Wi-Fi or LAN network, transfers bypass the public internet entirely, transferring at full local wire speeds.
